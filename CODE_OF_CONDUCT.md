# Code of conduct

This is a small tool for people who run a pick-and-place machine. Everyone
who takes part -- reporting a problem, asking a question, sending a change
-- is expected to be considerate and honest, and will be treated the same.

**Expected:** be clear about what you did and what happened; assume the other
person is doing their best; disagree about the code, not the person; accept
that a change may be declined with a reason.

**Not accepted:** insults, harassment, personal attacks, publishing someone
else's private information (including their board designs or customer data),
and sustained disruption of a discussion.

Anyone behaving that way will be warned once and then removed from the
conversation. Report a problem privately to the maintainer,
[koupypavel](https://github.com/koupypavel), by opening an issue with only
the words "conduct: please contact me" if you prefer not to write details in
public.

This text follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
